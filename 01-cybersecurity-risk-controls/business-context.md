# FinSecure Bank: Business & Technology Context

## 1. Business Overview

FinSecure Bank is a fictional U.S.-based financial institution that provides digital banking services to individual customers.

Customers use the organization's web and mobile applications to:

- View account information
- Review transaction history
- Transfer funds
- Manage their accounts

The organization relies on technology systems and third-party service providers to deliver these services.

Because FinSecure Bank processes sensitive financial and personal information, cybersecurity controls are necessary to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of its information and technology assets.

## 2. Technology Environment

The FinSecure Bank technology environment includes:

- Customer web application
- Mobile application
- Banking API
- Customer database
- Identity and access management system
- Employee laptops and endpoints
- Cloud infrastructure
- Cloud storage
- Logging and monitoring systems
- Third-party payment provider

## 3. Information Processed

The environment may process:

- Customer names
- Contact information
- Account information
- Transaction information
- Authentication information
- Employee information
- Internal business information

## 4. Cybersecurity Objective

The objective of this project is to identify cybersecurity risks within the FinSecure Bank environment and evaluate whether appropriate security controls are in place to protect the organization's information and technology assets.

The assessment will consider:

- Confidentiality
- Integrity
- Availability
- Identity and access management
- Data protection
- Secure configuration
- Vulnerability management
- Monitoring and detection
- Third-party risk
- Incident response

## 5. Project Scope

The initial assessment will focus on the following assets:

1. Customer Web Application
2. Mobile Application
3. Banking API
4. Customer Database
5. Identity and Access Management System
6. Employee Laptops
7. Cloud Storage
8. Third-Party Payment Provider

## 6. Project Approach

The project will follow a risk-based cybersecurity assessment approach:

1. Identify assets
2. Identify threats
3. Identify vulnerabilities
4. Assess cybersecurity risks
5. Identify existing controls
6. Identify control gaps
7. Recommend additional controls
8. Develop testing procedures
9. Evaluate evidence
10. Document findings and remediation recommendations
